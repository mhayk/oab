## What was rejected, and when to revisit

**`orchestration-platform`** — Three times the complexity budget and four times the money budget for a system with four orders of magnitude of headroom on one instance.

> Revisit when: More than 4 independently deployable services exist and a dedicated operations engineer is on the team.

**`cache`** — At 0.24 peak reads/second there is no measured read pressure to relieve.

> Revisit when: A single query exceeds 10 requests/second at over 50 ms, or database CPU is sustained above 60% for 3 days.

**`event-stream`** — 0.042 writes/second. A broker solves a throughput problem five orders of magnitude beyond measured demand.

> Revisit when: Sustained above 500 events/second, or 3 or more independent consumer groups need to replay the same stream.

**`read-replica`** — The primary is idle at this load; a replica would add a component and replication lag for no measured benefit.

> Revisit when: Database CPU sustained above 70% for 3 days after query optimisation.

**`search-engine`** — Full-text search in the existing relational database satisfies the browse and search requirement at this catalogue size.

> Revisit when: Search p95 exceeds 300 ms, or ranking requirements exceed what the database provides.

## Reliability

Target **99.99%** · achievable **99.5%** — ⚠️ **The stated target and this architecture disagree.**

99.99% permits 4.4 minutes of downtime per month. Every architecture within GBP 50/month has a human in the recovery path, and one person being paged, waking, and logging in exceeds that budget in a single incident. Reaching 99.99% requires multi-AZ redundancy with automated failover under a minute and no manual step, which is roughly GBP 400-600/month and an on-call rotation a single engineer cannot sustain. This design delivers about 99.5%. The requirements are inconsistent and the target should be revised or the budget increased.

## Open questions

- Is 99.99% a genuine business requirement, or a number chosen because it sounds serious? What actually happens at the fourth nine?
- If the target is real, is the budget negotiable to roughly GBP 500/month plus an on-call rotation?
- Would 99.9% (43 minutes per month) be acceptable? That is reachable at roughly GBP 120/month.
