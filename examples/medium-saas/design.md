## What was rejected, and when to revisit

**`event-stream`** — 21 writes/second and 2.3 jobs/second. A broker solves a throughput problem two orders of magnitude beyond measured demand, and the database-backed queue gives atomicity with the business write for free.

> Revisit when: Sustained above 500 events/second, or 3+ independent consumer groups confirmed to need replay of the same stream.

**`connection-pooler`** — 6 instances at a pool of 5 is 30 connections against a limit of 100 — 30%. Measured mean concurrency is 1.25 queries.

> Revisit when: Total connections exceed 80% of max_connections for 1 hour, recurring.

**`orchestration-platform`** — Two deployable types with no dedicated operations capacity. 4 complexity points against a budget with 4 remaining.

> Revisit when: More than 4 independently deployable services, and a dedicated platform engineer on the team.

**`search-engine`** — Database full-text search meets the current project and task search requirement.

> Revisit when: Search p95 exceeds 300 ms, or ranking requirements exceed what the database provides.

## Reliability

Target **99.9%** · achievable **99.9%** — ✅

43 minutes per month. Multi-AZ database with automated failover, multiple instances behind health checks, rolling deploys, and tested restores reach this. 99.99% would not be achievable: recovery still has manual steps.
